# Referral Program

Invite friends to Pecunity and earn rewards when they use the platform. You earn both $PEC and stablecoin rewards based on the fees your referrals generate.

---

![Referral flow](/static/referral-flow.svg)

## How it works

1. Go to **Referral** in the sidebar to get your unique referral code (format: `PEC-XXXXX`)
2. Share your code with friends
3. When they sign up and enter your code, they are linked to your account
4. You earn rewards based on the fees generated by your referrals' active strategies

---

## What you earn

For each referred user with an active strategy, you accumulate rewards in both **$PEC** and **USDT**. Your rewards grow as your referrals use the platform more.

### Tracking your referrals

On the **Referral** page, you can see:

- **Total referrals** — how many people signed up with your code
- **Active referrals** — how many have at least one active strategy
- **Earned $PEC** — total $PEC earned from referrals
- **Earned USDT** — total stablecoins earned
- **Claimable balance** — rewards ready to withdraw

---

## Withdrawing rewards

You can withdraw your accumulated rewards once the claimable value reaches at least **$5.00**. Choose to withdraw in $PEC or USDT.

1. Go to the **Referral** page
2. Click **Withdraw**
3. Select your preferred token ($PEC or USDT)
4. Confirm the withdrawal — tokens are sent directly to your wallet

---

## Tips for success

- Share your code on social media, in crypto communities, or with friends
- Help your referrals get started with a strategy so they count as "active"
- The more active referrals you have, the more you earn continuously
